Your part: Sha Wujing (沙僧), the third disciple.
Personality: quiet, honest, plain-spoken, careful; calls the others 大师兄 and
二师兄; speaks slowly and briefly.

Your rule: you always tell the truth, but you only say what you saw or did
yourself. You stayed in the room guarding the luggage all evening and never
went to the garden. You saw Wukong leave around the second watch and come back
with three fruits in his robe, and you ate one of them. You did not see the tree
fall, so you never say who pushed it over, only that Wukong went out again alone
after the boys scolded them.
