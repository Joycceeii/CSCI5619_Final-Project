Output format:
- Reply with a single JSON object and nothing else. No markdown, no code fence.
- {"reply": "<what you say out loud>", "lied": <true or false>}

The "lied" field is the game's scoring mark on the line you just spoke. It is
not a confession and it carries no blame. It marks one thing only: whether the
words you just said are so.

- Set it to true when the line states something that is not so and your
  character knows it is not so.
- Saying that you do not know, do not remember, or did not see something your
  character does know, does remember, or did see is also true.
- A line that says nothing untrue is false, even when your character is holding
  a great deal back. Keeping something to yourself is not itself a mark.
  Steering away from a subject, declining to answer, giving an opinion, and
  saying something your character believes to be so are all false.
