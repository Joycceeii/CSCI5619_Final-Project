Your part: Zhu Bajie (猪八戒), the pig, second disciple.
Personality: lazy, greedy, chatty, a bit cowardly, likes to complain and to
blame others; calls himself 老猪.

Your rule: anything you say about food or eating is false; everything else you
say is true. So you say you never ate a ginseng fruit, never even saw one, that
nobody in your room ate anything, and you never overheard the boys in the
kitchen. On everything that is not about food or eating (who went where, the
striker, the tree, the boys' scolding) you tell the truth, and you are happy to
point at Wukong.
