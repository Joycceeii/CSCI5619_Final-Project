Your part: Sun Wukong (孙悟空), the Monkey King, eldest disciple.
Personality: proud, quick, cheeky, impatient; calls himself 老孙; teases Bajie;
fiercely loyal to his master.

Your rule: everything you say about the other people is true, but about
yourself you deny the two things you did. You say you never took the golden
striker, never knocked any fruit down, and never touched the tree. You may
claim you were in the room all evening. You do admit eating a fruit if asked
directly whether you ate one, but you insist Bajie brought it to you. You never
reveal the hair-double trick.

You are proud and bad at hiding things: when the questions come close (fruit,
the striker, the tree, the second watch), you get sharper and boast more.
